What We Test & How We Do It

Every engagement is hands-on, thorough, and built around your release goals

✓

End-to-End Feature Validation

Complete workflow testing that mirrors how real users interact with your product, catching integration issues and edge cases before they reach production.

👤

UAT & Release Readiness

Pre-launch testing that confirms your software is ready for real users. We flag what's broken, what's confusing, and what could get in the way of a smooth release so you can go live with confidence.

🔍

Strategic Exploratory Testing

Creative, scenario-based testing that uncovers unexpected behaviors and usability concerns automation can't detect, protecting your reputation.

💻

Multi-Platform Verification

Testing across browsers, devices, and screen sizes to guarantee your users get a consistent, quality experience regardless of how they access your product. iOS and Android mobile testing included.

🔄

Change Impact Analysis

Systematic regression checks after each update to verify new features work while existing functionality remains stable and reliable.

📋

Actionable Quality Reports

Every bug is documented with clear steps to reproduce, severity ratings, and screenshots. Your developers can open the report and get straight to fixing, no back-and-forth needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our testing services

How does pricing work? +
We offer custom pricing tailored to your project needs, starting at $45/hour. Contact us for a detailed quote based on your specific requirements.
How long does testing take? +
Simple testing projects can be completed in 24-48 hours. The exact timeline depends on the complexity and scope of work. We'll provide a clear estimate after reviewing your project requirements.
Do you offer one-time projects or ongoing testing? +
Both. Some clients come to us for a single pre-launch pass, others use us on an ongoing basis as part of their release process. We'll work the way that fits your team.
Do you provide test documentation? +
Yes! We provide comprehensive test summaries, screenshots, videos, and detailed bug reports—everything you need to clearly understand the issues, their impact, and steps to reproduce them.
Can you integrate with our development workflow? +
Yes, we can work with your existing tools and processes. Please note that we may not always be available at the same time as your development team due to timezone differences, but we ensure clear communication and timely updates.
Do you test on real devices? +
Yes, we test on real physical devices to ensure accurate results. We can also use virtual environments for specific devices, browsers, or configurations upon request.
